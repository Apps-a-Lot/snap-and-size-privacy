Privacy Policy — Snap and Size

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Snap and Size shrinks photos you choose. This page says what happens to those photos, and what else the app may share.

At a glance

Your photos stay on your phone. You pick them. The app compresses them there. They are not uploaded.

Ads are separate. After you save, the app can show Google ads. Google may receive device and ad data. It does not receive your photos.

Questions: jeffvankirk07@gmail.com

The photos you select

The photos the app handles are only the ones you select.

Snap and Size processes those photos on your device. Shrinking the file happens on the phone. We do not upload the photos, and we do not have a server that receives them.

When you save, the new file stays on your device, in the place you pick. We do not keep a copy.

We do not ask you to make a Snap and Size account. We do not sell your photos. We never receive them, so we cannot use them to train anything.

Ads, and what Google may receive

Ads come from Google AdMob. They can appear after you save a photo, not while the photo is being shrunk.

Google may receive:

  • an advertising ID from your device
  • a rough location, taken from your IP address, not from GPS in this app
  • whether an ad was shown or tapped
  • basic diagnostic signals Google uses to run ads and limit fraud

That is device and ad data. It is not the photo you selected.

You can limit ad personalization in your phone’s settings. If you buy remove ads, that is a one-time purchase through Google Play. We do not get your card number. Google can tell the app the purchase went through, so ads stay off.

Children

Snap and Size is not made for children under 13.
